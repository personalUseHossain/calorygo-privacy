Privacy Policy

CaloryGo · Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information CaloryGo ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a Google account identifier. When you sign in with email, we collect your email address and the name you enter. We also assign your device an installation ID that identifies your account on our servers.

Profile and health-related information you enter. This includes your goal, sex, age, height, weight (including weight history), activity level and exercise frequency. We use it to calculate your daily calorie and nutrient targets.

Food and exercise logs. This includes meals and workouts you log by text, voice, barcode or photo, and the nutrition estimates generated for them.

Photos, camera and microphone. If you log food by photo or barcode, the app uses your camera. If you use voice input, the app uses your microphone and speech recognition. We only access these when you use those features, and only with your permission.

Usage and technical data. We record basic information such as when your account was last active, your credit balance and plan, and diagnostic data needed to keep the service running.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in on any device.
  • To calculate and display your calorie and nutrient targets and your progress.
  • To analyse the text, voice, barcode and photo entries you submit and estimate their nutrition.
  • To send you login codes by email.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Services that process your data

We use trusted third-party services to run the app. They receive only what they need to perform their function:

  • Google Sign-In to authenticate you if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Email delivery (Gmail) to send one-time login codes to your email address.
  • AI providers to interpret your food and workout entries (text, photos, voice transcripts) and to generate nutrition targets. Your entries are sent to these providers only to produce the result you requested.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers to store your account and logs.

These providers process data under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on secured servers. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limited access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal data and logs, except where we must keep limited information to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit: you can update your name, weight, height, age, goal and activity level in the app at any time. Your email cannot be changed.
  • Delete: you can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Permissions: you can turn off camera and microphone access in your device settings. Some features will then stop working.
  • Access and correction: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Health information notice

CaloryGo provides estimates for general wellness and informational purposes only. It is not medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before making significant changes to your diet or exercise routine.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. By using the app you understand that your data may be transferred to and handled in those locations.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

For questions, data requests or account deletion, email us at hello@mdhossain.dev.
